Following the 21st Military Operations Research and Modeling & Simulation Forum, Big x Reality continued preparing for the Taipei Aerospace & Defense Technology Exhibition (TADTE 2025), where it planned to present AI XR simulation and LVC-based training applications.

The announcement built on conversations with military specialists and industry partners during the forum. LVC (Live-Virtual-Constructive) brings together live, virtual, and constructive simulation approaches, supporting a wider range of training scenarios. Big x Reality aimed to share its modular training and cross-system integration work with exhibition visitors.

For professionals who could not attend the forum, TADTE provided another opportunity for face-to-face discussions. The company looked forward to introducing its simulation technologies and continuing conversations about intelligent, realistic training.
