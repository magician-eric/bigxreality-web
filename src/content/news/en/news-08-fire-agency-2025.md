On April 22, 2025, Hanson Chen, CEO of Global Power Technologies (Big x Reality), was invited to speak at the Ministry of the Interior Fire Agency's Training Center in Nantou, Taiwan. His presentation, “AI XR Multi-Dimensional Visualization Solutions for Smart City Disaster Prevention and Response,” explored how immersive technology can support emergency preparedness.

The talk focused on XR technologies—including augmented reality, virtual reality, and mixed reality—and their potential to improve disaster-response training efficiency and operational readiness, drawing on practical development and application experience.

Nineteen participants from 13 countries attended the session, representing Asia, Africa, Central and South America, and Europe. The original announcement listed India (Chennai), Eswatini, Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Chile, Ecuador, Paraguay, Peru, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Lithuania, and Micronesia.

Chen introduced applications including AR glasses with real-time firefighter positioning, a 5G AI Cloud XR disaster-response simulation platform, a 3D disaster situation dashboard, and an XR fire extinguisher training system. The presentation demonstrated how XR can combine data, scenarios, and mobile devices to enhance training realism, information visibility, and decision-making.

Global Power Technologies will continue integrating disaster preparedness with smart technologies to improve frontline safety and operational effectiveness.
