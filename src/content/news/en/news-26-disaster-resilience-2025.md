As communities face risks associated with extreme weather and earthquakes, preparedness needs to be developed long before an emergency occurs. Big x Reality is committed to discussing how education, technology, and cooperation can support broader disaster awareness.

The original social post referred to an exchange involving Hanson Chen and the Taiwan Disaster Prevention Industry Association. Its central message was that disaster readiness should not begin only after an incident. Public education, industry cooperation, and community preparedness all require sustained effort.

Big x Reality develops XR and simulation training technologies and continues to explore their relevance to disaster prevention education and scenario-based exercises. Repeatable virtual environments can help participants become familiar with procedures and consider decisions under different conditions.

Resilience is not built through a single device or event. It requires education, practice, and cooperation across society. Big x Reality will continue engaging with partners in this field.
