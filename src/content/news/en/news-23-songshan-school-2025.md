Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) worked with the Republic of China National Defense Education Association to bring immersive XR scenario training into the learning environment at Taipei Municipal Songshan High School of Commerce and Home Economics.

The activity was designed to go beyond a technology demonstration. Students were invited to consider how pressure, judgment, and teamwork affect responses to unfamiliar situations. The scenarios encouraged participants to think about when to act, when to hold position, and how to coordinate with teammates.

XR simulation offers a controlled, repeatable environment in which learners can reflect on their responses before facing complex situations in the real world. The event demonstrated one possible application of immersive technology in school-based education and resilience development.

Big x Reality will continue exploring ways to combine XR training with education and develop opportunities for scenario awareness, collaboration, and decision-making.
