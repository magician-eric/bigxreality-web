The Tokyo Metropolitan Government announced SusHi Tech Tokyo Global Startup Program 2024, held on May 15–16, 2024, at Tokyo Big Sight. The event brought together startups and businesses from around the world to exchange ideas on sustainable innovation.

SusHi Tech Tokyo, short for Sustainable High City Tokyo, highlighted the concept of “Sustainable New Values” through five themes: infrastructure, environment, life, culture, and impact. The program explored challenges including climate change, demographic shifts, infrastructure, and energy.

Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) joined Startup Island TAIWAN at the Taiwan Pavilion, participating under the “Impact” theme and presenting opportunities for Taiwanese technology innovation and international collaboration.
