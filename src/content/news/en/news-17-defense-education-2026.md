Civil defense education is not only about transferring knowledge. It also involves judgment, communication, and teamwork when people encounter unexpected situations. Big x Reality is exploring how XR-based scenarios can support learning in safe, repeatable environments.

The original social post referred to Polish defense education and the risk of misidentification under pressure. As the supplied material does not include independently verifiable sources for those specific international examples, this article does not present them as established research findings.

In Taiwan, Big x Reality has worked with an educational institution on XR scenario-based learning. The approach gives participants opportunities to consider threat recognition, incomplete information, and team coordination through practical exercises. Repeated scenarios can help learners review their responses and build experience.

Developing sound judgment takes time. Bringing scenario awareness and decision-making exercises into education is one way to explore more resilient responses to complex environments.
