Big x Reality introduced a range of AI XR military simulation applications in a social media video, illustrating the integration of VR, AR, MR, and geographic information systems (GIS) within training environments.

The featured modules included simulations involving Stinger missiles, mortars, and machine guns, as well as wargaming tables, battlefield analysis, speedboat training, and HMMWV vehicle simulation. Different modules can be arranged according to training objectives, from basic operational familiarity to more complex mission scenarios.

The original introduction also highlighted cross-location collaboration, data monitoring, and training feedback. Simulated environments and recorded results can provide instructors with opportunities to review procedures, team coordination, and decisions when planning subsequent exercises.

Big x Reality will continue exploring integrated AI XR approaches to military and law enforcement simulation training, with a focus on flexible and repeatable learning environments.
