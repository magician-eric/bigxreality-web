Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) participated in the Military Operations Research and Modeling & Simulation Forum, hosted by Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense at National Tsing Hua University, to demonstrate LVC synthetic battlefield training technologies.

The exhibits included a wargaming table system, a Stinger missile simulator, a .50-caliber machine gun operation simulator, and a helicopter pilot training system.

By integrating LVC (Live-Virtual-Constructive) technology, Big x Reality connects live, virtual, and constructive environments to demonstrate possibilities for future battlefield training platforms. The event also provided opportunities to exchange ideas with military officers and professional instructors.

We will continue advancing AI and XR applications in military training, supporting the evolution toward more digital, intelligent, and mission-oriented simulation.
