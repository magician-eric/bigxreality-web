As VR esports and immersive sports experiences gain attention, Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) is expanding its XR simulation technology into sports technology with a Shooting Sports XR Simulation System designed around Olympic clay-target shooting rules.

Combining XR technology with a simulated shotgun, the system recreates clay-target shooting practice and competition scenarios. It enables participants to train and compete from different locations, reducing time and space constraints.

The original report connected this development with interest generated by the taekwondo VR esports event at the 2023 Olympic Esports Series in Singapore and described the shooting system as a world-first solution. This wording reflects the source report and has not been independently verified.

To promote clay-target shooting, Big x Reality collaborated with the Taipei City Government Department of Sports, the Ministry of Digital Affairs' Administration for Digital Industries, and the Chinese Taipei Shooting Association on an XR clay-target shooting experience course. The course was planned for August 6–8, 2024, in an indoor classroom at Taipei Municipal Stadium, as part of a warm-up training program for the 2025 World Masters Games in Taipei and New Taipei City.
