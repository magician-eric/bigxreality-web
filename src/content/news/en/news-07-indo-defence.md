In June, Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) participated in the event identified in the original announcement as the “2024 Indo Defence Expo & Forum” in Indonesia, showcasing its Close-Quarters Battle (CQB) simulation training system to the Southeast Asian market.

From virtual scenarios to physical deployment, the modular system is designed for flexible use across different training requirements, supporting tactical response and decision-making practice.

During the exhibition, military, police, and defense-industry visitors showed interest in the simulation approach and ease of deployment. The event also drew coverage from METRO TV, CNN Indonesia, and YouTuber Auto Rich.

Post-event recap video: https://youtu.be/GUrKtH_TKjU

Big x Reality will continue advancing global tactical training applications through AI and XR technology and supporting the evolution of simulation-based training.
