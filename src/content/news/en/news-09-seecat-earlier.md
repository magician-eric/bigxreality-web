Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) was invited to participate in SEECAT (Special Equipment Exhibition & Conference for Anti-Terrorism) in Tokyo, Japan, in the year preceding the original announcement, showcasing Taiwan's innovation in security and defense technology and expanding its international engagement.

SEECAT is a specialized exhibition focused on counterterrorism and special equipment, bringing together government agencies, military organizations, law enforcement bodies, and security-industry decision-makers to exchange insights on emerging technology.

During the event, Big x Reality presented its immersive XR training systems and introduced the portable BXR Police Box training simulator in Japan for the first time. The system recreates law-enforcement scenarios and unexpected situations to support response and decision-making training for police and special units, attracting attention from professional visitors and prospective partners.

The exhibition helped the company better understand Japanese market needs and initiate discussions with local businesses. Big x Reality will continue developing efficient, safe, and scalable training solutions while pursuing opportunities for international cooperation.
