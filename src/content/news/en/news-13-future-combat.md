Big x Reality is gearing up for the Taipei Aerospace & Defense Industry Exhibition. Together with the Defense Industry Association, we plan to present advanced training solutions based on LVC (Live-Virtual-Constructive) simulation, enhanced by AI XR integration, modular design, and real-time physical feedback.

The exhibition is an opportunity to demonstrate approaches to combat readiness, simulation realism, and future training technologies for defense professionals and organizations interested in advanced tactical training.
