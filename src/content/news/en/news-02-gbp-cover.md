Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) was honored to appear on the second-day cover of GBP Daily News, published by GBP Aerospace & Defence during Defense & Security 2025 in Bangkok, Thailand.

GBP Aerospace & Defence covers companies and emerging technologies in the military and defense sectors. Being featured on its cover was an important milestone for our international outreach.

The coverage highlighted our SUT (Small Unit Tactical) AI tactical training system, including AI behavior modeling and real-time data analysis, immersive XR training environments, and a modular architecture designed for deployment in classrooms, training centers, or forward operating facilities. According to the original news summary, the system can adapt training difficulty to trainee performance and recreate interactive tactical scenarios.

The featured training modules also included UAV operation simulation and counter-drone engagement simulation. The UAV module focuses on reconnaissance, surveillance, and remote mission operation, while the counter-drone module addresses threat detection, aiming, response training, recoil feedback, and AI-driven behavior simulation.

The cover feature introduced Big x Reality's AI, VR, and XR simulation capabilities to a wider international defense audience. We will continue integrating advanced technology with defense training to help military and police organizations improve readiness in smarter and safer ways.

Location: Defense & Security 2025, Bangkok, Thailand.
