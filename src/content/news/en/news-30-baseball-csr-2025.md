Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) continued its support for rural education and youth development by sponsoring the 2025 Chiayi Hexing Cup National Baseball Invitational Tournament, helping create opportunities for young athletes to compete and learn.

The tournament provides a platform for teams to meet through sport, supported by the efforts of schools, coaches, families, and event organizers. Big x Reality thanks Hexing Elementary School and everyone involved for their work in giving young players a chance to demonstrate their dedication on the field.

For the company, supporting education and sport is one way to contribute to the community. Alongside its work in simulation technology, Big x Reality hopes to encourage learning and development opportunities for the next generation.
