At Defense & Security 2025 in Bangkok, our final major international exhibition of the year, Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) showcased a year of progress and innovation through its XR Small Unit Tactical Training System.

From tactical shooting and urban combat scenarios to UAV operation and counter-drone simulation, we welcomed military and police representatives, industry specialists, and partners from around the world for demonstrations and meaningful exchanges. One message stood out once again: technology enhances capability, while training creates safety. XR is turning preparation into a real operational advantage.

Throughout the exhibition, we presented our work through authentic technology demonstrations and hands-on experiences. Every discussion, observation, and encouraging response motivates us to keep moving forward.

We thank all visitors, supporters, and partners. Looking ahead to 2026, Big x Reality will continue developing stronger technology and more comprehensive modules to expand the possibilities of intelligent tactical training.

Big x Reality — Train Beyond Reality.
Location: Defense & Security 2025, Bangkok, Thailand.
