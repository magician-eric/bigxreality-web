Competition between China and the United States in technology, supply-chain restructuring, and the rapid rise of AI are reshaping industries worldwide. Yesterday's formulas for success may no longer apply; change is the only constant.

Hanson Chen, General Manager of Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies), shared his perspective: “In times like these, leaders must first break through their own boundaries. The era of hero-style leadership has passed. Only organizations capable of collective reflection and rapid adaptation can navigate uncertainty.”

He also described four qualities leaders should cultivate: integrity, through honest dialogue and trust; openness, through humility and receptiveness to different views; courage, through accountability and willingness to face challenges; and curiosity, through continuous exploration and innovation.

Chen also cited a reminder from Chien Lee-feng, former Google Taiwan General Manager: “When AI arrives, the first person who needs a revolution is yourself.”

Corporate transformation is not only about technology and market strategy. It also requires a renewed commitment to organizational culture and shared values. True leadership enables teams to act with clarity, composure, and resilience through mutual trust.
