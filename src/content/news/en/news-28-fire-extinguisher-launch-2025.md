Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) introduced an XR fire extinguisher training system that uses virtual reality to recreate fire-related scenarios, allowing participants to practice extinguisher procedures without a real fire source.

The original product announcement describes four types of fire scenarios and training with three extinguisher types. By combining simulated situations with realistic interactions, instructors can introduce different exercises and help participants become familiar with operating procedures.

The system also emphasizes training data tracking, portable deployment, and reduced reliance on consumables associated with live-fire practice. Simulation provides an additional repeatable training option alongside conventional fire safety education.

Big x Reality aims to make disaster preparedness more interactive through XR technology and will continue exploring applications in corporate training, education, and public safety.
