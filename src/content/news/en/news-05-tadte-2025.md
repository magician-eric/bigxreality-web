Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) participated in the 2025 Taipei Aerospace & Defense Industry Exhibition, presenting a range of simulation training systems built around AI and XR technologies.

The exhibits included an LVC (Live-Virtual-Constructive) synthetic training platform, a .50-caliber machine gun simulator, Stinger and Javelin missile simulators, the SUT Small Unit Tactical Training System, and tactical UAV simulation training.

The exhibition brought opportunities to exchange ideas with defense organizations, industry professionals, and international partners. Their feedback highlighted the growing importance of integrating live, virtual, and constructive training approaches into modern military preparation.

The showcase emphasized modular deployment, practical applications, and AI-based computation, reflecting our commitment to more digital, intelligent, and mission-oriented training.

Our next stop is Japan, as Big x Reality continues expanding its international engagement.
