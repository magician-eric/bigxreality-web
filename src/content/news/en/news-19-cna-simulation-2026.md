As equipment and mission environments become increasingly complex, training organizations face growing challenges in developing operational familiarity, situational judgment, and teamwork with limited resources. Big x Reality appreciates the Central News Agency's coverage of its XR simulation training work.

According to the company's original social post, the report featured the USV operation simulator, the SUT small-unit tactical training platform, and an integrated wargaming-table system. Together, these examples illustrate applications ranging from individual equipment operation to team coordination and information integration.

VR, AR, and MR technologies can provide safe, repeatable scenarios in which trainees become familiar with procedures, experience different mission conditions, and practice communication and decision-making. The value of simulation extends beyond reducing reliance on physical equipment and training space: it also creates opportunities for varied exercises and structured review.

Big x Reality will continue developing flexible approaches to XR-based training.
