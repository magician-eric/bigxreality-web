From October 1 to 3, Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) participated in SEECAT 2025 (Special Equipment Exhibition & Conference for Anti-Terrorism) in Tokyo, Japan, presenting the BXR SUT BOX Small Unit Tactical Training System.

Combining XR technology with simulated battlefield environments, the system supports UAV operation and counter-drone training simulations, offering scenarios designed around frontline training requirements.

Its modular architecture and immersive experience attracted interest and discussions with professionals from the defense, security, and training sectors.

Big x Reality will continue advancing XR applications for military and police training, expanding its international reach and helping organizations strengthen their ability to respond to complex threats.
