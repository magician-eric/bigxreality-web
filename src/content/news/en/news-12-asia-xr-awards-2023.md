The second Asia XR Golden Star Awards ceremony was held on August 11 under the guidance of Taiwan's National Development Council, with support from Startup Island TAIWAN and organization by the Taiwan Association for Virtual and Augmented Reality (TAVAR) and XR EXPRESS Taiwan.

The event partnered with XR and VTuber media outlet MoguraVR and Japan's XR Consortium, including the signing of a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with XR Consortium to encourage international exchange in the XR industry.

Japanese experts and industry representatives joined the event, including Shun Kubota, Executive Director of XR Consortium; Kuniyoshi Mabuchi, Representative Director of Metaverse Japan; and Takuya Nomura, a group director at Knowledge Capital. They participated in the international forum and awards program.

Fifteen awards were presented, and winning teams were expected to travel to Japan in December to participate in XR KAIGI, bringing Taiwanese XR innovation to an international audience.
