Big x Reality participated in the 49th National Chung Cheng Cup Shooting Championship, organized by the Chinese Taipei Shooting Association, and set up an XR shooting sports experience area at the Gongxi Shooting Range in Taoyuan.

The XR shooting sports simulator was designed with reference to Olympic trap shooting rules. Participants used VR headsets and realistic simulated firearms to experience aiming, shooting, and reloading procedures in a virtual environment. The demonstration introduced basic elements of the sport without requiring live ammunition during the simulation.

The original announcement noted feedback from participating athletes and previewed a further experience session at the same venue on November 23. Big x Reality hopes to make trap shooting more accessible through the combination of immersive technology and sports training.
