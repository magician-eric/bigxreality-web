faq:
- question: How is the speedboat simulation system different from an ordinary boat game?
  answer: The Speedboat Vehicle Training System is designed for boat handling and maritime mission training. It uses virtual waters and interactive operation so trainees practise specified boat handling procedures and situational judgement. This serious-game style of training focuses on practical skills rather than an entertainment experience.
- question: What maritime training content can be simulated?
  answer: Speedboat handling, navigation judgement, familiarisation with the maritime environment and mission-related scenario practice can be carried out according to the content the system supports. Actual sea states, navigation parameters and operating details must be confirmed according to the product configuration.
- question: Can people with no boat handling experience take the training?
  answer: The simulated environment can serve as a supporting tool for operating familiarisation and learning basic concepts, but beginner and advanced courses should still be planned by the training unit according to trainees' backgrounds and training objectives.
- question: Can speedboat simulation training replace real boat handling at sea?
  answer: Not completely. The simulation system is suitable for repeated practice of some procedures and situational judgement, but real sea states, the dynamics of a real boat and on-site operating experience still have to be gained through real maritime training.
- question: Is the speedboat training system the same as the USV Unmanned Surface Vessel VR Training System?
  answer: No. The Speedboat Vehicle Training System focuses on handling and mission training for crewed boats; the USV VR Training System focuses on unmanned surface vessel missions and a virtual operating environment. The two differ in who they train and in their objectives.
