faq:
- question: Is police VR training a game or formal police training?
  answer: This is a professional training system designed for police duties and on-scene response. The concept of serious games lets trainees practise in interactive simulated scenarios, but the training focus remains equipment operation, on-scene judgement, communication and appropriate response, not entertainment.
- question: How does the Full-Configuration Police Training System differ from the portable Police BOX?
  answer: The full-configuration system is suited to being planned as a more complete dedicated training environment and, depending on configuration, can be combined with body interaction equipment and scenario training; the Police BOX is characterised mainly by being portable, easy to move and flexible to deploy. The choice between them should depend on the site, training content and equipment requirements.
- question: Which police duty scenarios can be simulated?
  answer: Training related to equipment operation, situational judgement, tactical response and communication can be carried out according to the system content. In some courses, instructors can also take part in role-play so trainees practise verbal de-escalation and on-scene interaction. The scenarios actually available depend on the system configuration and course content.
- question: Is real police equipment used during training?
  answer: Depending on configuration, the system can use simulated police equipment and related interaction devices, such as training equipment corresponding to the operation of batons, pepper spray, conducted-energy weapons or handguns. This equipment is mainly for simulation training and should not be regarded as equipment with actual law-enforcement use.
- question: Can trainee performance be reviewed after training?
  answer: Yes. The training records and results that the system actually supports can be used for review, helping instructors give feedback. What can be recorded may differ between configurations, and this does not mean that every system has complete physiological monitoring or the same assessment functions.
