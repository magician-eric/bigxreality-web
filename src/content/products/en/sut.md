faq:
- question: Is SUT a game or professional tactical training?
  answer: SUT is a simulation system designed for small-unit tactical training. It applies the concept of serious games, combining mission objectives, interactive scenarios and tactical operation so trainees can practise repeatedly in a virtual environment. The game-based interaction is a training method; the core remains tactical skills, team coordination and situational judgement.
- question: What capabilities does SUT mainly train?
  answer: SUT is centred on small-unit action and can be used to practise communication between team members, tactical movement, covered coordination, close-quarters combat scenarios and decision-making during missions. Actual training content varies with the course and system configuration.
- question: Can SUT replace live-fire shooting or field tactical exercises?
  answer: Not completely. The value of SUT is in letting trainees first become familiar with tactical procedures, teamwork and situational response, and practise repeatedly in a controlled environment. Live-fire shooting, operation of real equipment and field exercises still need to be arranged according to training objectives.
- question: Can SUT be used together with drone or unmanned vessel training systems?
  answer: Some cross-system integration can be achieved depending on the project configuration. One integration application confirmed so far is that image information from the USV system can be sent to the SUT training environment. Connection methods for other drone, unmanned vessel or external systems must be confirmed based on the actual interfaces and project conditions.
- question: Which units is SUT suitable for?
  answer: SUT is mainly suitable for military or related professional units that need training in small-unit tactics, team coordination and situational response. Adoption should be assessed based on the number of trainees, training objectives, site conditions and equipment configuration.
