faq:
- question: Is wargaming a kind of war game?
  answer: Wargaming is different from recreational war games. Its main purpose is to help participants carry out tactical analysis, decision discussion and training by setting scenarios, force deployments and choices of action. It shares interactive learning concepts with serious games, but the focus is on wargaming and decision-making, not on winning or losing a game.
- question: What is the wargaming table mainly used for?
  answer: Through digital terrain, interactive maps and scenario simulation, the wargaming table helps users examine force deployment, movement and possible tactical developments. It can be used for military education, mission discussion and tactical planning exercises.
- question: Are the wargaming table and NOVA the same system?
  answer: No. The wargaming table is an independent product centred on interactive maps, tactical wargaming and information visualisation; NOVA is positioned as a larger-scale cross-system simulation integration solution. The two cannot be treated as the same system, and this does not mean they have a default connection capability.
- question: Does the wargaming table always require a VR headset?
  answer: The wargaming table can be operated interactively through a touch map and supports VR-related spatial visualisation. Whether VR equipment is actually required should be confirmed according to the mode of use and the system configuration.
- question: Can the wargaming table connect directly to all BXR training products?
  answer: That cannot be assumed. Data formats, interfaces and connection methods may differ between products; whether a product can be integrated with the wargaming table requires item-by-item confirmation of technical compatibility and project requirements.
