faq:
- question: Is USV VR training a game or professional unmanned vessel training?
  answer: This is a VR system designed for familiarisation and simulation training in unmanned surface vessel missions. It can use the interactive methods of serious games so trainees understand mission procedures and scenario information in a virtual maritime environment; entertainment is not its main purpose.
- question: Can this system directly control a real unmanned vessel?
  answer: The confirmed product positioning at present is a VR simulation training system, and it cannot be claimed to provide remote control of an actual unmanned vessel. Whether it can connect to physical equipment depends on separately confirmed system functions and project conditions.
- question: Can the USV VR system be used together with SUT?
  answer: Yes. In a supported integration configuration, USV video information can be sent to the SUT training environment so SUT personnel receive the corresponding maritime visual information. This does not mean that all functions of the two systems are interoperable.
- question: What is the difference between the USV VR system and the Speedboat Vehicle Training System?
  answer: The USV VR system focuses on mission awareness for unmanned surface vessels and related simulated scenarios; the Speedboat Vehicle Training System focuses on handling and navigation training for crewed boats. The two differ in what is operated, training purpose and system positioning.
- question: Which training uses is the USV VR system suitable for?
  answer: It is suitable for unmanned surface vessel mission familiarisation, maritime situational awareness and specific coordinated simulation training. Supported missions, interfaces and cross-system connection methods must be confirmed according to the deployment configuration.
