faq:
- question: Is VR fire extinguisher training a game, or can it really teach how to put out a fire?
  answer: This is a VR training system designed for education in fire extinguisher operation. It applies the interactive learning concept of serious games so trainees practise judgement and operation in virtual fire scenarios. The focus is on building basic procedures and concepts of equipment use, not simply a game experience.
- question: Which fire extinguishers can be practised with this system?
  answer: The training content includes simulated carbon dioxide, dry powder and foam extinguishers, so trainees learn appropriate selection and operation in different fire situations. Actual use must still follow local fire regulations and equipment labelling.
- question: What modes does VR fire extinguisher training have?
  answer: The system has three modes, Experience, Instruction and Test. Experience mode helps trainees become familiar with the scenarios, Instruction mode guides them through the operating procedure, and Test mode is used for scenario practice and reviewing results.
- question: Which fire locations can be simulated?
  answer: The training scenarios include a home kitchen, living room, office, high-tech factory, port and sheet-metal building, so trainees encounter fire response situations in different environments.
- question: Can VR fire extinguisher training completely replace operating a real fire extinguisher?
  answer: Not completely. VR is suitable for repeated practice of extinguisher selection, basic operating procedures and situational judgement, but the weight of real equipment, the spray response and on-site safety requirements still need to be supplemented through appropriate physical training.
