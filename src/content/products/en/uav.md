faq:
- question: Is this system a drone game or professional training?
  answer: This is a simulation training system designed for drone missions and counter-drone response. It can use the scenario design methods of serious games so trainees practise observation, judgement and response in virtual missions; entertainment or game scores are not its main goal.
- question: What is the difference between drone training and counter-drone training?
  answer: Drone training focuses on simulated content such as flight, navigation, observation and mission operation; counter-drone training focuses on identifying aerial threats, assessing the situation and practising the corresponding handling procedures in simulated scenarios. The two can be planned according to different training objectives.
- question: Can this system actually detect or shoot down drones?
  answer: This product is positioned as a simulation training system and is not equivalent to deployed drone detection, jamming or interception equipment. Counter-drone actions in training are virtual scenarios and procedural exercises and must not be regarded as providing physical counter-drone combat capability.
- question: Is a real drone always needed for training?
  answer: Simulation training allows selected operation and mission practice in a virtual environment without using a real drone every time. If the training objectives include operating a real aircraft, equipment maintenance or actual flight, corresponding physical training still needs to be arranged separately.
- question: Which training needs is this system suitable for?
  answer: It is suitable for simulation training in drone mission familiarisation, low-altitude threat awareness, base protection and related tactical response. Actual course content and equipment configuration must be confirmed according to the unit's needs.
