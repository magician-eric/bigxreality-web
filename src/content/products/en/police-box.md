- question: Is the Police BOX gaming equipment or police training equipment?
  answer: The Police BOX is portable equipment designed for police simulation training. Through interactive scenarios similar to serious games, it helps trainees practise duty judgement and related operations; the training goal is not entertainment, but improving professional skills and readiness to respond.
- question: What is the main difference between the Police BOX and the Full-Configuration Police Training System?
  answer: The Police BOX emphasises equipment portability and deployment flexibility and suits units that need to arrange training at different locations; the full-configuration system is better suited to establishing a dedicated immersive training environment. The training capabilities of the two must still be compared according to the actual configuration.
- question: Does the Police BOX need a fixed, dedicated training room?
  answer: The Police BOX has a portable design intended to reduce dependence on permanent, fixed training facilities. However, actual use still requires a space that meets equipment, safety and operating requirements; it should not be understood as usable directly at any location.
- question: What training can be carried out with the Police BOX?
  answer: Depending on configuration, training related to police scenario simulation, operating familiarisation and on-scene judgement can be arranged. The courses, interaction equipment and number of participants actually supported depend on the selected system version.
- question: Which units is the Police BOX suitable for?
  answer: It suits police-related units that need touring training, teaching across different sites or flexibly scheduled training activities. Suitability can be assessed at adoption according to how often it will be moved, the number of trainees and course content.
