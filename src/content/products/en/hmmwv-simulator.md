- question: What driving scenarios can be simulated?
  answer: Driving practice involving different terrain, road conditions and missions can be arranged according to the content the system supports. The actual scenes and the degree to which vehicle response is simulated must be confirmed according to the equipment configuration.
- question: Can people who have never driven a Humvee use it?
  answer: Simulation training can serve as a supporting tool for familiarisation with vehicle operation and procedures, but whether it is suitable as a beginners' course should be decided by the training unit according to its teaching objectives and operating safety requirements.
- question: Can the simulation system completely replace driving a real vehicle?
  answer: Not completely. Simulation training helps trainees become familiar with operating procedures and some situations beforehand, but the dynamic response of a real vehicle, road risks and the ability to operate a real vehicle still have to be built through physical training.
- question: Which units is this system suitable for?
  answer: It is suitable for units that need training in military vehicle operating familiarisation, tactical mobility preparation and mission-related driving. Specific equipment and site requirements should be assessed according to the system configuration.
