faq:
  - question: Is the Mortar Observation Simulation System a shooting game?
    answer: No. The system is designed for training in mortar observation procedures
      and fire correction, using simulated scenarios to help trainees become
      familiar with observation, judgement and communication procedures. Its
      interaction can apply the concept of serious games, but in essence it is
      professional procedural training.
  - question: Does this system train mortar gunners or observers?
    answer: The current product positioning centres on observation and the related
      fire correction procedures; it should not be understood as a complete
      simulation of physically firing a mortar. Target users and the scope of
      training should be confirmed according to course objectives.
  - question: Do mortar rounds need to be fired during training?
    answer: Simulation training allows specified observation and procedural content
      to be practised in a virtual environment without live firing every time.
      However, actual weapon operation and live-fire training should still be
      arranged according to formal training regulations.
  - question: What capabilities does this system mainly develop?
    answer: It mainly helps trainees become familiar with target observation,
      information judgement, procedures related to fire correction and the
      communication requirements of the mission. Actual training content is
      subject to the items the system supports.
  - question: Can courses be adjusted for different training needs?
    answer: Course configuration can be assessed according to the scenarios the
      product actually supports and the project conditions; not all training
      parameters can be adjusted freely. The specific scope of adjustment must
      be confirmed before adoption.
