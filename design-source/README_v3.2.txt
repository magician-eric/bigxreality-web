COLOR LAYERS
Brand   core: BxR Red #D02753 · Command Black #0B0F14 · Graphite #1E252D · White · Paper #F2F4F6
        text: Text Red #A51C40 (red text on light backgrounds only — not a second brand color)
        special accent: Command Brass #A8834F (corporate premium contexts only; not in sector materials)
Sector  Army #596B42 · Navy #173A55 · Air Force #5D8AA8 · Fire & Rescue #D2361E · Police #2E5C9A
        used as ACCENT (labels, key words, numbers, rules) and BLOCK (info blocks, bands, chart main series) only.
        No full-page / full-wall sector backgrounds. Logo, CTA and Reality Line always stay brand colors.
Asset   real uniform / camouflage / scene colors — never sampled into the palette. RD background #213B54 excluded.

Sector colors are generic associative colors defined by Big x Reality. They are not the official colors of
any nation, military branch, fire service or police force.

PRINT
CMYK values are FOGRA39 conversions. Vendors on Japan Color 2001/2011 must re-convert from sRGB. Proof before print (ΔE ≤ 3).
Large format: required ppi ≈ 3438 ÷ viewing distance (inches). Character images and key visuals are screen-resolution
mockups; print, posters, roll-ups and the 9 m backdrop need RD re-rendering (see Asset_Inventory_v3.2).
