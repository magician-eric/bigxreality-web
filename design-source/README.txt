COLOR LAYERS (v3.1)
- Brand:  BxR Red #D02753 (only corporate red) · Text Red #A51C40 (text on light only) · Command Black #0B0F14 · Graphite #1E252D · Command Brass #A8834F (auxiliary)
- Sector: Army #596B42 · Navy #105A75 · Air Force #5B8FD0 · Fire & Rescue #C2410C · Police #323B6E (each with Deep / Light / Tint)
- Asset:  real colors of uniforms, camouflage, scenes and photos — kept as-is, never sampled into the palette.
- Retired: Red Light #E8577A, Steel Blue #3D5A73, Field Amber #D99A2B, Command Navy.
- Excluded: the RD reference-render background #213B54 is a 3D workspace default, not a CIS color.

Sector colors are generic associative colors defined by Big x Reality. They are not the official colors of any nation,
military branch, fire service or police force.

IMPORTANT
- Character images come from BxR character reference renders (figures 240–450 px tall), AI-upscaled 2×.
  They are fine for screen use. For print, posters, roll-ups or the 9 m backdrop, re-render from the original 3D models.
- Key visuals are synthetic mockups (BxR characters + rendered training environment). Replace with real training photography or engine renders before external release.
- Character C (OPFOR / NPC) must always appear in the simulation layer and never as the brand hero.
- Character × sector: A → Police, B → Army. Navy / Air / Fire pages use no A/B hero; C may appear in any sector's simulation layer.
